Shaping Future Educators

Since our establishment in 2009, Shri Swami Samarth College of Education has been dedicated to cultivating passionate and skilled teachers. Located in Barshi, we provide a nurturing environment that blends theoretical knowledge with practical teaching experience.

Our institution takes pride in its affiliation with Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Solapur University, ensuring our curriculum meets the highest academic standards.

Academics

Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.)

Our flagship B.Ed. program is designed to develop comprehensive pedagogical skills, subject mastery, and an understanding of educational psychology. We prepare our students to excel in modern classrooms and educational leadership roles.

  • Duration

    2-Year Full-Time Program

  • Intake Capacity

    50 Students per Batch

  • Affiliation

Our Facilities

Central Library

A well-stocked library featuring academic texts, journals, and reference materials essential for teacher education and research.

Computer Science Lab

Modern computing facilities equipped with the latest software to enhance digital literacy and technological integration in education.

Science Laboratories

Fully equipped laboratories for physical and biological sciences to facilitate practical demonstrations and experiments.

Sports Grounds

Expansive sports facilities including dedicated grounds for Kho-Kho, Kabaddi, and other athletic activities to promote physical education.
